Privacy notice

Your enquiry details.

This calculator collects the information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, postcode, calculator inputs, any requested appointment time and an optional energy bill uploaded with an appointment request.

The information is used by Mark Fitzpatrick to provide your solar assessment, respond to your enquiry and confirm a requested telephone appointment. It is not sold to third parties.

Calculator information may be sent to a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. An uploaded energy bill is stored in protected cloud object storage and an unguessable retrieval link may be included with your enquiry. A backup of calculator details may also be stored in your own browser. Google and hosting services may process information under their privacy terms.

A bill is used only to review your tariff, usage and seasonal energy pattern so a more accurate recommendation can be prepared. Please redact account numbers or other information you do not want to share. Uploaded bills are retained only as needed to handle the enquiry and can be deleted on request.

You can ask to access or correct your information, or ask that it be deleted, by emailing mark.fitzpatrick@classaenergy.com.au or calling 0434 151 237.
